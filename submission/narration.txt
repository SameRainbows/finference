AI software can grow while its margins quietly collapse. Revenue is visible immediately, while inference cost arrives later in a blended provider invoice. Finference is the AI margin control plane that connects every request to customer revenue, governs safer routing decisions, and converts the same ledger into billable usage.

This is not a static concept page. The judge path creates a real Neon Auth session and opens Aurora Labs, a tenant-isolated workspace backed by live Neon Postgres. Policies, audit evidence, API-key digests, events, rate-limit counts, and meter batches survive reloads and deployments.

Aurora has twenty-eight thousand five hundred dollars in AI revenue and thirteen thousand seven hundred fifty-one dollars in inference cost. That is a 51.8-percent gross margin, below the company's 60-percent target. Finference attributes the gap to specific model routes rather than hiding it inside a provider total.

The margin agent proposes moving seventy-two percent of low-complexity support traffic to a more efficient model, while premium handling remains in place for escalations and risky cohorts. Before activation, Finference replays ninety-six thousand two hundred twenty-one historical requests. Semantic quality remains at 94.8 percent, latency improves, and escalation precision is preserved.

The decision is bounded and reversible. Only a workspace owner or administrator can activate the atomic proposed-to-active transition. Quality must remain above 94 percent, P95 latency below fifteen hundred milliseconds, and provider errors below two percent.

After approval, Finference writes the actor, policy scope, traffic share, evidence, and rollback predicates to Postgres. Reloading proves the state is durable. Protected cost falls to ten thousand eight hundred eighty-seven dollars, gross profit reaches seventeen thousand six hundred thirteen dollars, and gross margin rises to 61.8 percent.

Operators can create a workspace ingestion key. The plaintext appears once; only its SHA-256 digest, prefix, tenant, and revocation state are stored. A test request then passes strict schema validation, persistent rate limiting, tenant authentication, and database idempotency before joining the authoritative economic ledger.

The same ledger powers billing. New events are aggregated into economic units under a deterministic source watermark. The meter batch remains durable if Stripe is unavailable, and the flush identifier becomes the replay-safe Stripe meter-event identifier when credentials are installed.

The governance feed shows the real approval and API-key actions created by this authenticated workflow. Across the product, tenant isolation, HMAC verification, human approval, minimal data collection, and replay-safe webhooks are first-class.

Finference is designed as a recurring-revenue SaaS with tiered subscriptions and usage-based economic units. Neon database and authentication are live today. The repository also implements Backboard workspace threads with persistent memory and Stripe Checkout, Billing Meters, signed webhooks, and provisioning. Those external adapters are reported honestly as adapter-ready until account credentials are installed.

Finference turns delayed AI cost into a governed, revenue-aware operating loop: observe, optimize, approve, and bill—so AI growth improves margin instead of quietly destroying it.
