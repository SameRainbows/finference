AI software can grow while its margins quietly collapse. Revenue is visible immediately. Inference cost arrives later, blended across providers and disconnected from the customer and feature that created it.

Finference is an interactive AI margin control plane. It turns every model request into an economic event joining cost, revenue, tokens, latency, feature, model, and customer in one auditable ledger.

This production-shaped demo has twenty-eight thousand five hundred dollars in AI revenue. But model cost is growing too fast, and gross margin has fallen to fifty-two percent—below the company’s sixty-percent target.

The working dashboard exposes unit economics by route, model, feature, and customer. Its signed ingestion endpoint validates events and handles retries idempotently. The same event model is designed to drive transparent usage billing.

The margin agent has found a specific leak. Most support turns are low complexity. It proposes routing seventy-two percent of eligible traffic to a more efficient model, while preserving the premium route for escalations.

For credential-free judging, the public deployment uses a deterministic agent fallback. The included Backboard adapter can switch to live persistent memory when configured—remembering quality floors, service-level exceptions, previous rollbacks, and operator preferences.

Before anything changes, Finference replays historical traffic. Semantic quality remains above the ninety-four-percent floor. Latency improves. Escalation precision is preserved. Projected savings are two thousand eight hundred sixty-four dollars per month.

The decision is explainable and reversible, with a bounded traffic share, evidence, a human approval gate, and automatic rollback if quality, latency, or provider reliability regresses.

Approve the recommendation, and the control plane updates immediately. Inference cost falls to fourteen thousand two hundred ninety dollars. Gross margin rises to sixty-one point eight percent. Model economics change, and the approval enters the audit trail.

Finference is more than a concept page: the workflow, simulation engine, financial calculations, API boundaries, tests, and deployment are functional. It is an honest vertical slice—not yet a multi-tenant production service. Persistent storage, authentication, live traffic, and external billing credentials are the next operational layer.

The product already includes a Stripe Checkout adapter, tiered subscriptions, security controls, continuous integration, and a documented scale-out architecture.

Finference gives AI teams a governed path from raw token usage to protected revenue—so growth improves margin instead of quietly destroying it.
